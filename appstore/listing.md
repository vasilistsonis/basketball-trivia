## Description  (1206/4000)

```
Two teams. One phone. Fourteen questions to settle who actually watches the games.

Hoops Trivia is pass-and-play: name your teams, pick your jersey colours, then hand the phone back and forth while the scoreboard keeps count. No accounts, no ads, nothing to buy.

THE BOARD
Five categories, each with slots worth 1, 2 or 3 points. Take the easy bucket or go for the three.

- Geography: arenas, cities and courts, from the pros to European club basketball.
- History: the moments, the records, the dynasties.
- Logo: name the team from its crest.
- Who's Missing?: an iconic starting five with one player gone. Fill the gap.
- Guess the Player: follow a career path and call the name.

POWER-UPS
Each team gets two per game. Double Up puts 2x points on one question. Fifty Fifty wipes out two wrong answers for half the points. Spend them early or save them for the final possession.

A DIFFERENT GAME EVERY TIME
Nearly 400 questions in the bank, drawn at random, so you never see the same board twice.

ARENA SOUND
Crowd noise, the ref's whistle, the swish of the net, and haptics on every call. Mute it when the game is on in the next room.

Needs an internet connection to load questions.

Drop dimes.
```
